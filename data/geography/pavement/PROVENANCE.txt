Only pa_tiedown, pc_tiedown and pa_shoulder_f triangles are selected. The source
union is clipped within 85 m of OSM aprons 105366843, 105366846 and 105366849 and
within app metre bounds [-550,-350,1300,700]. Complete current mapped building,
grass and water polygons, existing apron interiors, and former Terminal 1's
historical outline are subtracted. The resulting 278232.590872 square metres
are split into 46 parts on a 200 m grid without simplification. This dated source
supplies pavement at three approximately 29 m apron-to-taxiway gaps; it is not
evidence of the exact current pavement edges or a current airport survey.

clip-metre-geometry.json preserves the exact crop and subtraction input, using
global app east/north metres (not longitude/latitude GeoJSON). Its OSM-derived
geometry is attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL 1.0:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Original OSM response URLs/hashes are recorded in ../heathrow-west.json.

BTG decoding follows the published format in FlightGear/SimGear sg_binobj.cxx;
the decoder here was independently written. Float ECEF vertex offsets are added
to the double bounding sphere centre, converted to WGS84 geodetic coordinates,
then projected with the app's radius 6371008.8 m, origin (-.46,51.47) and fixed
cos(51.47) easting scale. Independent iterative geodetic conversion agreed with
the Bowring implementation within 1.43e-14 degrees for all 29340 vertices;
ECEF roundtrip error was at most 2.84e-9 m. Source absolute elevation is not
added to the intentionally flat scene surface.

Reproduce with an isolated Python 3.9+ environment containing Shapely 2.0.7,
numpy 2.0.2 and mapbox-earcut 2.0.0:
  python derive_pavement.py /tmp/heathrow-derived-pavement.json
The output reproduces the coordinates, holes, roofCoordinates and roofTriangles
of all 46 flightgear-2013-t3-pavement-* features exactly. Roof triangles use
counter-clockwise east/north winding; Unity reverses once for upward normals.
Each triangle union matches its clipped polygon within 1e-6 square metres.
