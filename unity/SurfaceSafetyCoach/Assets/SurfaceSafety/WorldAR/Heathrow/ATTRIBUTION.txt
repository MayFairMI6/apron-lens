ApronLens conversion: selected 5A and interiour meshes, triangulated faces, preserved UVs, centred and rotated the model toward a custom apron. Converter: scripts/convert_heathrow_ac.py.

The surrounding apron, stand markings, service equipment and placement are custom simulated training scenery. They do not reproduce current Heathrow operations or surveyed stand geometry.
