Running or sharing the app

The native iOS Simulator app has been built and tested. Running it again requires both the installed app and the local scenario/model service. The repository's TEAMMATE_SETUP.md and CHECKPOINT1_CURRENT.md explain the Apple Silicon Mac, Unity, Xcode and model setup. Both people can prepare narration from the recording without installing that environment.

AirfieldLens is positioned as portable scenario practice and review for learners, not an operational control workstation. The phone suits a focused flight/report/checkpoint task; a tablet or larger display is the recommended format for a broader traffic overview. This is a design recommendation, not a completed controller-usability finding. Checkpoint 3 should compare phone and larger-screen use with representative learners.
