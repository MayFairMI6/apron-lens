Suggested narration:

“ApronLens helps a learner follow a changing airport situation and work out what the available reports actually establish. We are showing selected excerpts from a recorded native iOS Simulator run. The exercises focus on an obstructed ground route, an arriving aircraft waiting for an occupied stand, and delayed baggage affecting a departure estimate.

The learner starts with a short briefing. During practice, they watch the aircraft, select a flight, read incoming reports and ask the assistant questions. There are three checkpoint moments in each lesson. At each checkpoint, the scene, assistant and history are hidden, and the learner answers a four-choice question. Feedback explains the answer using the reports available at that moment. The lesson ends with a debrief. The scored learner flow is separate from the guided recording used for these movement clips.

Here, TRN103 is waiting for Stand B. The important question is whether the stand is confirmed available. A plan for the occupying aircraft to move is not enough. At this answer's cutoff, the report still says the stand is occupied.

Now a later report confirms availability and releases the exercise hold. The answer changes because the evidence has changed. Selecting the flight gives the question its context, and the cutoff tells us which reports were available to answer it.

Sources opens the reports supporting that particular answer. Recent reports can include updates received afterward. That difference helps the learner check both the evidence and its timing.

The local Qwen model selects relevant report facts. A separate checker checks the claims against eligible facts, and the app displays the accepted summary. The responses in this recording came from actual requests; they were not replaced with rehearsed answers.

I will hand over to my teammate to show how the moving scenario is driven and what we have evaluated.”

Running or sharing the app

The native iOS Simulator app has been built and tested. Running it again requires both the installed app and the local scenario/model service. The repository's TEAMMATE_SETUP.md and CHECKPOINT1_CURRENT.md explain the Apple Silicon Mac, Unity, Xcode and model setup. A teammate can prepare narration from the recording without installing that environment.

No TestFlight build has been published. The current service listens only on the original Mac's loopback address. A phone-distributable build would need device signing and an accessible authenticated service, followed by App Store Connect/TestFlight setup. For this video, the shared recording is the immediately usable route. Simulator verification does not establish the assignment's physical-smartphone requirement.
