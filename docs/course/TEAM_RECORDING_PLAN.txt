Useful source-video excerpts: 0:43-1:35 stand availability and change; 5:35-6:05 fire answer; 6:50-7:03 post-containment update. Locate the visible Sources expansion in the recording before choosing its exact cut. Never claim to have clicked a control live when showing a recording.
Handoff: The final section separates the working demonstration from the research evaluation.

Presenter D - teammate 3: results, limitations and next checkpoints (6:00-8:00)
Present the frozen M1 study: 40 distinct turns in 17 families across four scenarios, reused under three context conditions. Qwen3.5-9B, Qwen3-VL-4B and a deterministic baseline were compared. Full-AR scores were 24/40, 16/40 and 16/40 respectively. Images were off. Removing pointing reduced the smaller model from 40% to 25%; tracking context did not help in this setup. Acknowledge the small sample and uncertainty. Contrast the scope of the saved demo evidence: six accepted requests, not a general accuracy estimate. Explain M2 visual evidence and the planned turnaround task, then M3 integrated evaluation. State physical-phone verification remains outstanding.
